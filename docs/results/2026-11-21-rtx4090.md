hardware. Numbers are transcribed from the pod's terminal output.

## Attacks (`attack/run_pod.py`)

Eve is in the same org as Alice, sharing the org system prompt. K = 20
candidates; 10 guessing trials, 5 extraction trials.

| | Stock | Labels | Chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extract a 6-digit account number from cached-token counts | **6 of 6 digits, every trial** | **0 digits** | — |
| Guess the account among 20, cached-token channel | 0.7 | 0.1 | 0.05 |
| Guess the account among 20, timing channel | 0.6 | 0.0 | 0.05 |
| Median latency gap, right vs wrong guess | **41.85 ms** | −0.29 ms | — |

## Reading the numbers

- **Stock SGLang leaks, and the leak is practical.** A same-org co-tenant
  recovered the full account number digit by digit in every trial. The 42 ms
  timing gap is large enough to exploit without cached-token reporting.
- **Labels close the channel and keep legitimate sharing.** Eve recovers
  nothing; the timing gap disappears; 0.1 on the guess is one coin-flip hit
  in ten trials, consistent with chance. Alice's own hits and the shared org
  prompt still work.
- **Stock guessing at 0.6–0.7 rather than about 1.0 is most likely a
  measurement artifact.** Each trial adds about 12K tokens of probes, which
  fills the 4090's KV cache, so LRU eviction can drop the victim's entry
  before Eve probes it. The harness now flushes the cache between trials;
  rerun to confirm.
